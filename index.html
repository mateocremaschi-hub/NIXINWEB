What you get

What the report contains.

Every finding is tied to a single module: what the fault is, how much hotter it runs than the rest of its string, and where that module sits on the site.

01 / REPORT

Module-level addressing

Each finding carries its block, tracker, row, string and module position, counted the same way your crew counts in the field. Delivered as CSV and PDF.

02 / CLASSIFICATION

Fault type, not just heat

Cell hotspot, bypass diode, open string, whole-module and tracker-level events are separated by the shape of the thermal signature. A misaligned tracker is reported as one event, not as 50 module defects.

03 / EVIDENCE

Conditions on the record

Irradiance, wind, sky and gimbal angle logged per capture and checked against IEC 62446-3. Radiometric frames retained, so any finding can be re-opened later.

Process

We start by listening.

No two sites are run the same way. We ask how yours works first, and shape the report around it — your naming, your priorities, the format your team already uses.

STEP 1

Your brief

A call to understand what you need to find, how your team names and records assets, and what the report has to contain to be useful to you.

STEP 2

Your site on paper

Send whatever you already have — plans, spreadsheets, however your team names its blocks and rows. We work with your documents as they are, and tell you up front if anything is missing.

STEP 3

Survey and analysis

We fly the whole site during the hours when the sun is strong enough for the readings to count, then compare each panel against the ones it works alongside — so dirt or a passing cloud never ends up counted as a fault.

STEP 4

Your report

Every finding checked against its visible image, then delivered in the format we agreed — within a week of the last flight.

Scope

What every survey includes.

  • Full-site radiometric survey with the flight conditions logged per capture
  • Module-level report with physical addressing — block, tracker, string, module
  • Fault classification by thermal signature, with severity graded by ΔT
  • Tracker and string-level events grouped as single events, not repeated per module
  • Report delivered as CSV and PDF, with the radiometric frames retained
